## CON-0001 assessment

**Existing proposition:** the starter register describes an unattributed graphic as implying that three ordered layers are necessary for tension and omission defeats the effect. This is the register’s description, not a verified transcription of the original. Source: supplied `REGISTERS.md`, line 15; pilot specification, lines 16–22. `SRC-0001` supplies no original evidence.

**Competing propositions:** topic C-01 / pattern C-01 propose conditional added value; topic C-02 proposes non-necessity within the bounded game context. These are different claims. Success for the conditional recipe would not establish necessity or sufficiency. Lack of direct support for necessity does not, by itself, prove non-necessity.

**Assessment (`inference`):** the universal should not be used as design guidance. The inspected perception study does not measure tension; the film experiment challenges an unrestricted three-stage account but is not an isolated-cue gameplay falsification; the sparse-exploration creator account challenges compulsory layering but measures no player response. Neither of those latter sources proves that context is absent: selective quiet may itself create an expectation. There is no established empirical conflict between “ground context” and “remove excess sound.” They can coexist.

**Proposed register treatment:** retain `CON-0001` with status **unresolved — necessity unsupported; narrower contextual hypothesis retained for testing**. Do not mark “resolved,” “proven,” or “disproved in all games.” Preserve original provenance uncertainty, the bounded meaning of tension, and first/repeat/access distinctions. Link topic C-01/C-02/C-17 and pattern C-01/C-06; keep SRC-0001 separate from SRC-0002/SRC-0004/SRC-0006.

**Evidence needed:** the original source and its intended scope, if obtainable; a genuinely matched, locally recognized-context comparison in the specified interactive task; separate emotional and comprehension measures; and relevant access/repeat checks. One clear counterexample would defeat a precisely stated universal, but still would not prove a best technique. No such new game experiment ran here. **Owner:** domain research prepares evidence; Coordination manages the contested-claim record; Doug decides completed-pilot acceptance. No canonical register edit made.
