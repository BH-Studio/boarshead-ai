Second n=40, ages18–23, passive rollercoaster/dot task; 31 completed all blocks, MISC paired dz=.693,p<.001. Central-only reference cue, changed task and attrition constrain causal interpretation. Draft preserves these limitations and does not treat null as equivalence. No raw-data reanalysis.
