## Primary-source scope checks

Sources below were checked at the pertinent original paper or first-party documentation on the review date, rather than relying on search summaries. These are spot checks of adopted claims, not complete literature reviews.

- F06-001: [Harris, Hancock and Scott](https://markhancock.ca/pmwiki/uploads/Publications/p350-harris.pdf), printed pp. 355–359. The study used 34 participants in 17 acquainted pairs. Character/interface differences limit attribution. Super Kirks was hypothetical interview material, not a tested self-sufficient condition. TOP-01 C-02 and PAT-01 appropriately retain those limits; necessary coordination is not universally beneficial.
- F06-002: [Kwak, Blackburn and Han](https://syslab.cs.washington.edu/papers/lol-chi15.pdf), data collection/Table 1 and H4.2. Reported-match selection prevents all-player prevalence estimates. The recording/sample-unit discrepancy and negative-statement outlier exclusion are disclosed. The candidates do not infer silent players' demographics, uniquely establish the authors' evolutionary explanation, or propose skill-based profiling.
- F06-003/004: [Season 10 account](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24061006/defense-matrix-update-season-10-and-beyond) and [October 15, 2024 patch account](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/overwatch-2-retail-patch-notes-%E2%80%93-october-15-2024/932243). ## Practical logic, arithmetic and evidence labels

PAT-01 distinguishes UI availability from authoritative transition enforcement. Its high-confidence statement is explicitly conditional on all relevant paths enforcing the rule. Revocation before commitment, changed target/scope, recovery ownership and stale authority are separated. A role matrix is not a security audit. Collusion, displacement and legitimate cooperation costs remain open.

PAT-02 separates historical intention, current facts, pending request and committed result. Current-state revalidation remains necessary at commitment, even after an accurate display. Knowledge entitlement applies to actor/cause/timestamp disclosures; player notes are untrusted input. The brief neither invents a goal nor automatically replays an old action. The synthetic example is arithmetically consistent: current cost 5 and inventory 4 is unaffordable, despite old cost 3. Proposed enough/exactly-enough/not-enough probes preserve accommodations and do not infer understanding from a single correct answer. No statistical or algorithmic performance result was fabricated. These were document-logic checks, not executed product tests.

Empirical claims are bounded to studies; practitioner guidance and historical policy descriptions are not relabeled outcome evidence. Proposed matching, permission and re-entry benefits remain hypotheses. Conditions and counterexamples include trusted collaboration, chosen betrayal, queue scarcity, discovery-oriented play, ordinary-state sufficiency, unmaintainable freshness and burdens transferred to other players.
