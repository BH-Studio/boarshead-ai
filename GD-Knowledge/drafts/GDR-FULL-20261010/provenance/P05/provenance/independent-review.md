## Findings and dispositions

### P05-R1 — Feasibility is not willingness (moderate; corrected)

The localization topic's resource probe and message-unit pattern said that repeating a refusal indicated failure to transfer, after stock changed from insufficient to sufficient. This does not distinguish misunderstanding from a deliberate choice to preserve resources. The exact selected F01 pattern explicitly separates permitted action from preferred action. This is a real dependency-transfer inconsistency, despite the author self-review claiming the example preserved the correction.

Changed both examples to request an explicit feasibility prediction and available quantity before feedback. Only a repeated false impossibility judgment is contrary to the demonstrated relation; refusal alone is not. Added an equivalent accessible response route and reminders that correct answers may reflect guessing, memorization or other cues. No participant result is claimed. The packet addendum overrides its historical self-review claim. This repairs the diagnostic without changing the pattern's design purpose or synthetic arithmetic.

## Content and applicability assessment

### Audio design: TOP-01, C-01–C-12

Decision scope covers all assigned facets, including the otherwise easy-to-omit sonic identity, occlusion and range distinctions. Cue contract, information chain and recovery are correctly labeled synthesis. Engine features do not become demonstrated perception or performance. HRTF and geometry documentation does not certify localization accuracy; mono is not presented as spatial equivalence. Range processing is distinguished from gain, with no medical exposure level or universal compressor setting. Essential information is not silently subordinated to realism, ambience categories or artistic tension. The environmental-identification countercase is useful: background texture may itself be the task.

The existing contextual-anomaly pilot remains separate and weak. Unsupported necessity is not turned into proof that ambience never helps. Public SOMA material corroborates the creator's stated contrast goal only. No independent gameplay replication is claimed. W02 truth/expiry transfer is now declared consistently. Proposed listening and mix comparisons specify useful alternatives and separate mechanism tests rather than bundling all changes. Efficacy is still untested.

### Music design: TOP-02, C-01–C-12

Separates runtime mapping, composer intent and player experience. Original Plut/Pasquier statistics and sample accounting were rechecked; the exact comparison supports adaptation versus static-neutral in this small study, not congruent superiority over inverse or no-score. Composition and mapping differ, remote/in-person conditions vary, the modified measure and unverified tension constructs limit transfer, and no raw-data/multiplicity reanalysis is claimed. The text's cautious interpretation is appropriate.

Cowen is separate listening research, not gameplay. Country subgroup counts, brief stimuli, within-country heterogeneity, exposure and perceived/experienced limits are retained. The published Methods inconsistency is real, not an author's arithmetic error here. Motifs and rhythm remain coverage hypotheses, with no invented optimal repetition, tempo, timing window or neurological law. Fixed, adaptive, intentionally contrasting, performative and no-score choices all receive serious treatment. Validation separates policy from musical-material polish and local task truth from affective preference.

### Localization: TOP-03, C-01–C-14

Goes beyond string fitting: semantic relation, commitment, ambiguity, rendering, voice takes, input/focus and revisions are distinct. Unicode scope is accurately limited to bidi conformance and permissible higher-level protocols; it neither translates text nor determines whole-interface mirroring. Shaped width and line breaking are properly separated. ICU supports whole-message grammatical freedom, not automatic translation quality. Pseudolocalization remains an early diagnostic. Caption access guidance is not a universal reading-speed or expansion budget.

The word-puzzle countercase prevents functional equivalence from becoming silent design/canon authority. Selected takes and scripts require reconciliation; historical IGDA cultural stereotypes and cost multipliers are explicitly rejected. Cultural consultation and actual locale QA are not claimed. The corrected feasibility probe now aligns with its F01 dependency. Stable identity is appropriately treated as traceability, not semantic approval.

### State-valid musical transition: PAT-01, C-01–C-07

Distinct conditional recipe: request, pending choice, irreversible commitment, audible arrival and recovery. It has prerequisites, adjustable variables, exits, a worked reversal, simpler comparator, and falsifiable failure criteria. Rechecking before irreversible submission is realistically qualified: scheduling documentation does not prove safe cancellation or prevent all changes after commitment. The recipe explicitly supports limited guarantees and simpler structures when the target cannot meet them. Intentional irony, retrospective expression and player performance are genuine do-not-use cases. No direct empirical validation of this combined policy exists in the inspected corpus. Retain as hypothesis-led draft.

### Locale-resilient message unit: PAT-02, C-01–C-07

A practical authoring/review pattern rather than another generic redundancy pattern: coherent meaning, variables, contextual constraints, selected assets, invalidation after revision and switching recovery. Handles both over-fragmented and over-broad unit boundaries, missing versus fallback versus checked variants, and the cost of metadata. The comparator can legitimately remove fields or the whole pattern. Exact word-form tasks and changed commitments require design-owner decisions. Feasibility correction prevents a coercive or invalid learning assessment. The combined workflow's defect reduction remains a hypothesis; associated documentation and LQA advice cannot establish a measured effect.
