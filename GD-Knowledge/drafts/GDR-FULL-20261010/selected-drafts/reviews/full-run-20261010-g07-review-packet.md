## Author synthesis and what the evidence permits

The tactical-RPG file centers **local decisions coupled to persistent party capabilities or identity**. The tactics file centers **local coordination of action, position, timing, information and objectives**, without requiring an RPG campaign. These are proposed working boundaries. Levels, permadeath, grids and purely alternating turns are not imposed as definitional requirements. The inspected documents provide contrasts, not a definitive genre taxonomy or measured audience expectations. Fully nonviolent and no-level shipped boundary cases remain incompletely verified; synthetic examples are not substituted for that missing evidence.

The strongest provisional pattern is **Consequence-preserving roster recovery**. When a game has explicitly promised continuation after a loss, audit and preserve a route to the necessary future capability without pretending to restore the original person or every outcome. Feasibility is a rule-dependent inference; better comprehension, meaningfulness and experience are low-confidence hypotheses. A chosen terminal-loss mode, deliberately irreparable relationship, or task built around irreplaceable scarcity can defeat the recommendation. This is not a universal fail-forward rule.

A second important conclusion is conditional rather than prescriptive: legal-order preview, outcome forecast, accessible information, cancellation, state reversal and human knowledge are different objects. Choose a policy after specifying the intended uncertainty, task and commitment. G07 transfers the relevant reviewed distinctions but creates no duplicative preview/undo pattern. No public source inspected establishes a universal benefit for maximal forecasting, unrestricted rewind or irreversible defeat.

## Author self-review — not independent review

**Reviewer identity:** the same assistant authoring G07. This is an adversarial author pass, not a separate agent, independent expert, original experiment or Gate C approval. Null reviewer metadata is retained.

**Strongest recommendation challenged:** capability-route recovery can preserve continuity without erasing consequences. The strongest contrary case is a deliberately unforgiving expedition whose defining task is keeping the original scarce crew alive. A replacement role can negate scarcity even when dialogue remembers the lost person; a different route can negate a strategically meaningful failure even when rewards are reduced. A player may prefer a clear ending or replay over a contrived substitute. Therefore the pattern is limited to an explicit continuation promise and should be rejected when it undermines the chosen task. “Meaningful loss” is not claimed as a result.

**Structural leap challenged:** a plausible alternate route is not evidence that a full campaign remains solvable. It can depend on a missing permission, late deadline or resource already spent. The recipe requires a bounded precondition audit and reports untested states; the illustrative traces are neither executed state models nor player tests. Even a successful future structural audit would not establish comprehension or enjoyment.

**Genre boundary challenged:** persistence alone could make the proposed tactical-RPG definition overinclusive. Named units, equipment and unlocks occur in many tactics games. The file therefore asks whether character/party capabilities or identity materially organize continuing decisions and keeps neighboring/hybrid classifications contested. It does not assert that a market tag is wrong or that the shipped no-level/noncombat range has been verified. G06 must be reviewed before a parent/subgenre relationship is canonically resolved.

**Evidence and selection bias challenged:** readily accessible official pages describe feature intentions and attractive possibilities; they are poor sources for failure rates, affected populations and disappointing outcomes. Multiple successful products do not provide causal triangulation. The original permadeath paper was not read in full, so no empirical conclusion is borrowed from it. Exact-build evidence is missing for most cases, and Japanese translation has not received independent bilingual review. These limits remain in every affected standalone file.

**Duplication and ownership challenged:** the new pattern may collapse into a longitudinal application of existing objective and difficulty guidance. Its proposed distinction is cross-scenario capability reachability after persistent roster change, not a new generic “fail forward” or “safe undo” rule. Coordination/independent review should remove or merge the new artifact if that distinction is not useful, preserving its temporary identity and an explicit supersession relationship rather than silently renaming it. Existing P01/P02 cue-pattern overlap is untouched.

**Access, ethics and production challenged:** a technically viable replacement route can be inaccessible, culturally incoherent, stigmatizing, narratively inconsistent or too costly to maintain. The documents contain no universal accommodation setting or audience partition. The task tests keep access needs separate from desired stakes and preserve F01/W02's corrected unchanged-rule transfer distinction. No numerical accessibility value is imported without its normative scope.
