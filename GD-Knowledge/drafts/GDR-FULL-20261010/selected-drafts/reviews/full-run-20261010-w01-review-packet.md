## Research brief used for W01

**Narrative decision:** What kind of influence and consequence is offered, and which authored/systemic structure can support it without assuming that more branches are inherently better?

**World decision:** Which local rules and relationships require consistency and evidence, which histories or viewpoints can conflict, and how much world detail is justified by the intended experience?

**Character decision:** What actions, goals, constraints, relationships or changes should the player interpret, and when are minimality, stable commitment, refusal or ambiguity better alternatives than extensive biography or a compulsory arc?

The source strategy combined primary theoretical treatments, original experiments, creator accounts, practitioner authoring explanations and official accessibility/localization guidance. Search targeted both narrative-heavy and action/coordination contexts. Counter-search targets included immediate-feedback limits, the difference between nonsignificance and equivalence, limits of goal-based explanations, refusal and restricted protagonist power, plural world accounts, minimal/nonlinear narrative, and the maintenance costs concealed by reuse. Search snippets, abstracts and source bibliographies were discovery routes only.

Material was inspected before its use in candidate claim records; this report presents those records with the synthesis. The available body is not a systematic review, exhaustive search, meta-analysis or independent replication. No comprehensive database-search coverage, publication-bias estimate or universal design law is claimed.
