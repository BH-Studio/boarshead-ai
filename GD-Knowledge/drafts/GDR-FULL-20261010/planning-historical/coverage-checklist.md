## Ownership and overlap checks

- F08/F09/F10 ↔ W08/W09/W10: Foundations owns mechanics and simulation; Worlds owns spatial/scenario orchestration; Presentation owns player-facing communication.
- F01/F03/F04/F05 ↔ P03/P07/P08/P09/P10/P11: Separate intended experience and learning/challenge design from controls, information delivery and access; keep shared causal claims in one canonical home.
- F06/F07/F12/F15 ↔ MMO/CCG/idle and other genres: Shared progression/economic mechanisms are neutral topics; monetization and engagement implications are conditional and ethically reviewed, not genre stereotypes.
- W01/W03/W04/W12 ↔ P01/P02/P05/P12: Separate narrative meaning, performance/presentation and cultural interpretation; dramatic intent does not establish audience response.
- F10/F13 ↔ W02/W07/W11: Procedural rules, coherence/continuity and narrative-state concepts overlap; runtime implementation and game canon remain outside this research scope.
- F11 ↔ party/social deduction/MMO/MOBA and competitive genres: Communication, moderation and team dynamics need shared references plus distinctive genre constraints; multiplayer is also a facet.
- parent/subgenre labels: Platformer/precision platformer, shooter/tactical shooter, RPG subgenres, strategy/tactics, adventure subgenres and simulation subgenres need explicit deltas rather than duplicate summaries.
- cross-family/hybrid labels: Metroidvania/action adventure, tactical RPG/tactics, roguelike/roguelite/deckbuilder, survival horror/psychological horror/stealth, extraction/survival/shooter require multi-label tradeoff checks.
- cozy/educational/sandbox and platform facets: Roster mixes promises, purposes and mechanics. Preserve approved labels, define usage, and avoid turning VR/mobile/open-world/live-service into extra genre files without justified scope change.

## Essential gaps to watch

- Full taxonomy breadth is not unlimited encyclopedic completeness. Each broad heading may need multiple bounded briefs; report uncovered subtopics and source-access gaps explicitly.
- Audience/population variation, disability access, cultural context and production constraints must be assigned at brief level; a single accessibility/ethics/localization chapter is insufficient.
- Online trust/safety, moderation, exploit incentives, competitive integrity and network/platform effects need scoped treatment under F11/F15/P13 where material; security implementation is not automatically authorized.
- Saves, interruptions, re-entry, assist options, comfort and input/display constraints are easy to omit when research emphasizes dramatic or combat examples.
- Technical claims need engine/platform/build context and freshness checks; research must not silently become a harness architecture or implementation project.
- Coverage status needs evidence of actual files and claim-level review, not simply started chats, uploaded outlines, completed templates or genre-audio pilot notes.
- The historical starter lists zero released entries and unrun host tests; it is not reliable evidence of the current live state. Reconcile actual receipts and current canonical revision before reporting progress.
