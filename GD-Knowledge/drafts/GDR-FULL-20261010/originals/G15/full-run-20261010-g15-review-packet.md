## Author self-review — not independent review

**Reviewer role:** the same assistant that authored this packet. Specialist viewpoints below are lenses, not independent agents, experiments or corroborating reviewers.

**Strongest survival-crafting recommendation challenged:** diagnose information versus supply before changing acquisition friction. An opposing case is a player who values gathering itself or a small production unable to maintain a truthful live survey. A diagnosis can become an elaborate justification for removing a chosen activity. Response in this draft: the survey is optional and hypothesis-level, a notebook is a genuine alternative, maintenance/freshness are prerequisites, and desired activity—not shorter time—is a deciding measure. Remaining weakness: no direct causal crafting study or player observation validates this diagnostic procedure.

**Strongest soulslike recommendation challenged:** separate recovery components before adopting a convention. An opposing case is a tightly integrated expedition in which approach, supplies, imperfect build and encounter form one deliberate commitment; isolating them in a test can change the experience itself. Response: the whole expedition is an explicit comparison and a valid desired activity. The draft rejects both universal short retries and universal costly retries. Remaining weakness: no direct within-build comparison establishes when separation is a useful abstraction rather than an artificial one.

**Strongest pattern recommendation challenged:** a known-resource query can preserve discovery. A technically correct filter can still reveal an unknown cave through result density, an absent item through a count, or another player's activity through location changes. Response: separate identity, scope, precision and freshness; test side-channel inference; retain the no-aid and notebook alternatives. Remaining weakness: one production's historical mechanism is a weak foundation for general benefit. Independent review may reasonably demote this to a genre adaptation or merge it with a related pattern.

**Evidence lens.** Official descriptions are not live behavior, intent is not effect, the questionnaire is selected self-report, and the preprint's corpus excludes key contrary populations. Raw data and coding are unverified here. No popularity or completion statistic is used as causal proof. An inaccessible scholarly lead is not substituted with model memory.

**Access/ethics/localization lens.** The drafts preserve required information while questioning unwanted disclosure; they do not conflate lower damage with access or use assistance as a public moral label. Cultural/ecological proposals remain questions, not claims about communities. Representative user validation and direct language testing are missing.

**Production/retrieval lens.** More settings and finer-grained information require coherent state/version policies. Existing shared recipes are not duplicated to fill a quota. Relative links inside the G15 packet are checked locally; external dependency paths are marked unresolved. No evidence establishes that a live Project host retrieves these files correctly.

**Author disposition:** retain as three coverage-open drafts for independent Gate C review, with no recommended release.
