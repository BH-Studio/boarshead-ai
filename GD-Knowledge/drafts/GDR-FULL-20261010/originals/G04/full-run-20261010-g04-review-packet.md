### Principal evidence-calibrated conclusions

**Shooter:** the important distinctions are among execution demands, spatial/task context and correct versus apparently correct feedback—not military theme or one input device. Contextual assistance evidence supports caution about transfer, not a universal preferred assist.

**Tactical shooter:** preparation, information and recoverability are different dimensions. Short time-to-kill is not a validated tactical-quality recipe, and winning is not sufficient evidence of sound decisions.

**Extraction game:** the excursion/retention boundary is separate from weapons. Legible consequences can coexist with intended uncertainty; baseline recovery and contested exits impose genuine tradeoffs. No direct causal loss/churn/compulsion result was established.

These conclusions are inferences or scoped findings as classified in the candidate ledgers, not human-approved studio decisions. No material recommendation is classified as a formal standard. No gameplay efficacy is claimed for either new pattern.
