## Findings and evidence-to-application limits

The two topics supply conditional decision processes rather than universal tuning rules. Decision design separates access to information, uncertainty about the world, resource feasibility, valued differences, opportunity cost and commitment. Challenge and difficulty separates the demand being tested, the reason for failure, the resulting setback and the available recovery route.

The strongest actionable proposals are **hypotheses**: compare a genuine displaced alternative when it is being overlooked, and use bounded disclosed adaptation only when a target signal, player contract and invariants are defensible. Neither proposal is validated merely because related studies or game options exist. Detailed causal links, alternatives, exit conditions, failure cases and falsifying proposed tests are in each pattern.

There is contrary evidence to simple maxims: the inspected option-count meta-analysis does not show a universal overload effect; reversible choices are not always associated with greater outcome liking in the inspected non-game study; Juul’s exploratory failure work does not establish a causal failure-frequency optimum; adaptation’s reported immersion effects do not establish equivalent performance or trust benefits. Relevant populations, methods and limits remain attached to the corresponding claim records.

**Claim classes used:** empirical-evidence, practitioner-heuristic, observed-example, inference and hypothesis. No F02 substantive claim is certified as a formal standard. XAG pages are vendor guidance. P01’s corrected normative scopes are preserved as transferred review constraints, not converted into fresh native-game numerical recommendations. The research dispatch is a studio decision about work authorization; none of the pattern recipes is an approved studio design decision.

**Strongest challenge to PAT-01:** The opportunity-cost corpus largely concerns hypothetical decisions.
