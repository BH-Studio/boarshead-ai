## 4. Synthesis and genre separation

The following are **inferences and hypotheses**, not settled genre laws. Entry claim links identify exact classifications and limitations.

| Design question | Survival horror | Psychological horror |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary unresolved problem | How to survive or recover under consequential constraints. | How to interpret self, relationships, responsibility, knowledge or reality. |
| Useful unit of analysis | Commitment, alternative, cost, exposure and recoverable state. | Evidence, viewpoint, supported interpretation, action and disclosure. |
| Strongest proposed starting point | Audit the survival decision and depleted states in retry-supported exploration. | Preserve usable access to intended actions while allowing unresolved meaning. |
| Strongest opposing case | Chosen costly failure or permadeath can be the intended activity. | Deliberately vague systems or a known inevitable outcome can be preferable. |
| Misleading shorthand rejected | No weapons; retro camera; low resources automatically create fear. | No combat; random contradictions; diagnosis itself defines the genre. |
| Shared adjustment | An honestly described alternative consequence policy may help a desired activity. | The same option may reduce execution barriers without removing disturbing themes. |

Locators: SH C-01/C-09–C-11/C-18; PH C-01/C-07–C-10/C-15; PAT C-03–C-08. These are not mutually exclusive labels. A hybrid needs an explicit scene-level priority and transition policy; neither genre automatically wins. Platform, multiplayer mode and tone were treated as facets rather than additional entries.

**Limits with direct contrary evidence:** the SOMA texts disagree on attack behavior; only their common nonlethal characterization is retained without further verification. The environment paper reports both differences and nonsignificant manipulations; it cannot supply a universal horror palette. Relevant primary locators are source 03/04 Safe Mode answers and source 09 Sections 7–8 / Tables 2–3. No product’s success is used as causal proof.
