# Idle/incremental

**Research candidate · version 0.1.0 · coverage open.** This entry concerns decisions whose consequences unfold across reduced interaction, accumulation, automation and possible resets. It does not supply a retention-maximization recipe, a healthy-play-time threshold or a claim that large numbers establish depth. No executable game, economy simulation or player test was run.

## Definition and contested boundaries

For G20, **idle** emphasizes progression or system activity during periods without continuous player action; **incremental** emphasizes accumulating and reinvesting resources to change subsequent possibilities or production. Record these properties separately rather than assuming a marketing label guarantees either one. This is a working analytical convention, not an approved taxonomy revision. [IDL-C01]

Alharthi and colleagues’ original artifact analysis covered 66 idle and 10 non-idle games, with two researchers examining each game. Their taxonomy nests incremental within idle and describes an interaction spectrum. It also uses a no-death/no-game-over boundary that should not be universalized from a historical, availability-biased sample. G20 uses the spectrum as a question framework, not proof that contemporary examples all obey that definition. [IDL-C02; GDR-SRC-20261010-G20-007]

Distinguish **background execution**, **calculation on return**, and **network-independent availability**. A game can continue calculations while open without supporting progression while closed; “offline progress” need not mean every feature works without a connection. These are logical product distinctions to verify per build, not claims about a particular untested product. Similarly, an ordinary waiting timer does not by itself establish a sustained idle structure. [IDL-C01]

Prestige/reset is an optional structure, not a requirement. A finite automation puzzle, an indefinitely expanding economy, a desktop companion and a mostly observational system can make different promises. Platform, monetization and multiplayer remain facets; they do not receive new genre identities in this batch. An illustrative manual reinvestment game is a boundary question for the combined label, not evidence that the inspected taxonomy used that broader definition. [IDL-C01]

## Custom research instructions

Study **what decisions remain after automation**, not merely how fast a number grows. Identify the resource sources, uses, limits, conversions and uncertainty; when the player can intervene; what happens without intervention; and what a returner must understand before acting. Distinguish real opportunity cost from pressure created by inaccessible information or threatened prior investment. [IDL-C03]

For original studies, separate artifact coding from player outcomes. For production accounts, record the optimization objective and commercial context. For game observations, require platform/build, a reproducible state and the exact absence/return or reset sequence. Do not infer current caps from old patch notes, regard a simulated continuous rate as a complete economy, or treat prestige popularity as evidence of wellbeing or depth. [IDL-C03]

Subsequent research should compare finite versus endless progression, active versus low-interaction phases, online/background/return-calculated rules, multiplayer responsibility and no-prestige designs. Seek negative cases: a dominant purchase that removes choice, a reset that adds only repetition, a return summary that becomes an obligation cue, and a zero-player experience whose value is observation rather than optimization. These are research targets, not claims that G20 tested them. [IDL-C03, IDL-C11]

## Evidence anchors and representative range

**Production model, not a universal economy.** Pecorella describes generators, costs, multipliers and prestige, using explicit production/cost models and an income-focused optimization criterion. The model simplifies some timing behavior and its bulk-purchase formulas have stated restrictions. It offers inspectable design reasoning, not a trial showing that the proposed curves are enjoyable. No source spreadsheet was executed. [IDL-C04; GDR-SRC-20261010-G20-008]

**Return calculation status.** The official Melvor Idle v1.3 preview announces a progress bar for remaining offline calculations. Its stated aim concerns visibility while computation proceeds. This is a documentary example of a proposed feature, not a G20-observed build or evidence that a full return-summary pattern works. [IDL-C05; GDR-SRC-20261010-G20-009]

**A counterpressure to throughput as the only objective.** Rusty’s Retirement’s developer-authored description includes automated helpers and a Focus Mode that slows crop production. That declared option illustrates a different product priority; it does not prove that working alongside the game improves productivity or reduces distraction. [IDL-C06; GDR-SRC-20261010-G20-010]

The named cases were selected to expose different decisions, not to represent market prevalence or commercial success. User ratings, community tags, sales claims and unobserved gameplay are not evidence in this entry. [IDL-C03]

## Player promises and characteristic structures

Possible promises include seeing a small system grow, choosing a plan and inspecting its consequences, expressing ownership through an arrangement, solving resource bottlenecks, or enjoying an ambient companion. Do not assume every player wants mathematically optimal income or an endless horizon. Elicit the desired relation between attention and consequence. These are candidate needs, not measured population segments. [IDL-C07]

A proposed structure is active establishment → automation → a consequential choice → time for consequences → inspection and adjustment. The return can be a decision point rather than a demand to clear a queue. The long horizon may add production relationships, unlock qualitatively different options, culminate in a finite goal, or reset a subset of state. An alternative is deliberate observational play with very little decision depth; assess it against that promise rather than manufacturing extra management. [IDL-C07]

## Mechanisms and practical choices

All predicted experiential benefits in this section are **hypotheses**. Mathematics can establish an outcome under explicit assumptions; it cannot by itself establish that someone will value the outcome. [IDL-C08]

| Decision | Proposed mechanism and usable choice | Limits, costs and alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Manual work to automation | Removing repeated execution may shift attention toward planning or observation. | Automation can also remove the enjoyable action or educational practice. Retain a valued active route only without making repetitive input an access toll. |
| Accumulation and storage | A limit can make rate, storage and absence horizon interact. | A cap can instead encourage unwanted check-ins. Compare persistent uncapped progress, a finite task or a different bottleneck. |
| Long-horizon choices | Alternative plans may perform differently under different goals and time horizons. | More currencies do not guarantee meaningful alternatives. Expose dominated or nearly irrelevant upgrades during design review. |
| Prestige/reset | Losing some state while retaining other state can create a new planning problem. | If the only change is a larger multiplier, replay may be repetitive. Compare an optional new branch or a finite ending without erasing progress. |
| Return information | Settled state, changes and stopping reasons may support the next decision. | Too much recap is a cost. An ordinary current-state view may suffice; no universal modal is recommended. |
| Respectful stopping | A clear and usable stopping contract may reduce decisions made under mistaken obligations. | Transparency alone does not resolve an exploitative or unwanted rule. Shared systems may impose legitimate costs on others. |

Treat **elapsed wall time**, **eligible simulated time**, **active production time**, **gross output**, **consumption** and **net change** as separate quantities when the rules require them. Multiplying the current rate by absence duration is only valid under an appropriate constant-rate model without incompatible caps, depletion, transitions or rule changes. A different model needs a different explanation. [IDL-C09]

For prestige, specify exactly what is lost, retained, unlocked and uncertain before confirmation. Compare forecasts with the real state assumptions; do not promise a precise recovery time when changing strategy or random outcomes make it unreliable. The consequence preview and refusal path are proposed applications of reviewed safe-exit reasoning, not a new approved G20 prestige pattern. [IDL-C08, IDL-C10]

## Application process and worked decision

**Diagnose:** identify the player's chosen objective and the point at which meaningful intervention disappears. **Model:** use the smallest explicit resource model that captures the actual decision. **Compare:** change one source of complexity at a time, including a no-reset or less-interactive alternative. **Evaluate:** inspect understanding, preference, access burden and errors separately from accumulated output. **Revise:** remove a system whose benefit exists only in a growth chart or a longer session. This is a proposed process, not a performed economy evaluation. [IDL-C08]

**Illustrative example: rate versus capacity.** A player has one upgrade credit and an empty store. Baseline production is one unit per minute, with capacity twelve. Upgrade A doubles production but retains the capacity; B retains production but raises capacity to thirty. No consumption, random events, travel or other transitions occur during the absence. At four minutes A produces eight stored units and B four; at twenty minutes A stores twelve and B twenty. These are exact arithmetic consequences of this invented model, not balance targets. [IDL-C09]

A player planning a longer absence has a reason to consider capacity rather than simply choosing the largest displayed rate. But the model does not prove a deep game: repeated future choices might all favor one option, and a person may prefer a visual upgrade instead. Do not judge a knowingly chosen lower-output option as misunderstanding. To test comprehension, ask what each plan permits before asking which the person prefers. [IDL-C08, IDL-C09]

**Counterexample:** remove the cap and all differing downstream effects; doubling production now dominates an upgrade that does nothing useful for the chosen goal. Adding impressive numbers has not restored a tradeoff. **Another:** a prestige resets the store and restores the identical purchase order faster, without a new decision or desired observational experience. It may be acceptable spectacle, but “strategic depth” remains unestablished. **Another:** a fully predictable, setup-only display supplies the desired ambient experience; adding frequent choices could make it worse. These are illustrative falsifying cases, not named-game accusations. [IDL-C08]

## Hybrid conflicts

In a **cozy hybrid**, automation and calm presentation may coexist with attendance pressure. Test perceived obligation and choice rather than reading the art style as evidence. In an **educational hybrid**, automatic resource growth can remove the practice that supposedly teaches; explicitly choose whether the target is execution, forecasting or interpreting a model. A successful simulation forecast is not automatically broad mathematical transfer. In a **multiplayer hybrid**, a personal stop command may not pause the shared world; describe teammate effects and obtain appropriate coordination review rather than promising cost-free unilateral exit. [IDL-C10; [cozy game](cozy-game.md), [educational game](educational-game.md)]

## Accessibility, ethics, localization and production

Proposed access checks include a usable alternative to prolonged repeated input, deliberate inspection of changing values, stable focus through updates, readable unit labels and a way to obtain detail without reading every production tick. Do not assume a flashing reward cascade communicates an outcome to every player, or make an access-preserving automation route depend on payment. These are proposed design boundaries, not claims about specific products. [IDL-C12]

Localization must preserve units, magnitude notation, separators, signs, net versus gross quantities and plural forms. Very large abbreviated numbers can obscure a meaningful difference; consider exact values or a comparison explanation on request where exactness matters. Test the full decision, including assistive reading, rather than the isolated number string. No particular notation system is declared universally best. [IDL-C12]

A truthful return contract has production implications: save compatibility, authoritative accounting, rule-version changes, clock anomalies, calculation completion, duplicate settlement and recovery from uncertainty. These are requirements to raise with system owners, not engine code or a prescribed architecture. Verify power and resource behavior before claiming that continuous background rendering is lightweight or sustainable; G20 made no such measurements. [IDL-C12]

A long absence should not be treated as consent to reminders, a purchase offer or a lecture about missed output. Conversely, a voluntarily selected risky persistent activity may be part of the desired experience. Inspect actual choice and foreseeable costs without assuming either safety or coercion from the genre label. [IDL-C12; reviewed F07-TOP-02 and F07-PAT-02]

## Research questions and validation

**Proposed, not performed.** Test whether the rate/capacity choice remains understandable when a person changes absence horizon, objective or access route. Compare a current-state-only presentation against a compact reconciliation with optional detail, holding the underlying outcomes constant. Include new players, returning players and relevant input/reading access needs; do not recruit only expert optimizers. [IDL-C11]

Ask for predicted consequences separately from preferred action. Observe incorrect affordability decisions, misunderstanding of what stopped, accidental resets, unwanted interruptions and assistance needed to compare quantities. Ask about chosen attention, pressure, interest and regret without converting logged time into a health measure. Retention may be collected only as a separately justified outcome, not as proof of player benefit. [IDL-C11]

Before any study, specify the local objective, meaningful comprehension improvement and acceptable burden, with a justified analysis plan and participant protections. Reject the pattern or narrow the target audience if added information does not reduce the relevant errors, impairs access or makes a desirable peripheral experience feel compulsory. Reject a claimed strategic improvement when alternative plans remain behaviorally indistinguishable or one option dominates under the intended goals. No player tests, prototypes or simulations were performed by this batch. [IDL-C11]
