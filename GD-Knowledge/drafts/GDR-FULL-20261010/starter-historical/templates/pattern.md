# Pattern name

Template only. Use a memorable descriptive name, not a claim of universal effectiveness.

## Purpose
What player-facing problem does this solve? What outcome is intended?

## Context and prerequisites
When might it apply? What player knowledge, system behavior, genre expectations, technology or production resources does it assume?

## Mechanism and evidence
Explain the hypothesized causal chain and identify which links have evidence. Classify the pattern's recommendation and attach claim IDs.

## Recipe
Provide ordered, actionable steps, adjustable variables and decision points. Source numerical values or label them proposed local parameters. Include an exit condition when prerequisites fail.

## Worked examples
Show at least one contextualized example at sufficient detail to apply the recipe. Distinguish observed games, creator accounts and illustrative inventions.

## Tradeoffs and alternatives
Explain benefits, costs, opportunity costs, interacting techniques and simpler alternatives.

## Failure cases
Describe the conditions that produce boredom, confusion, unfairness, overload or other harm. Give observable warning signs and possible corrections.

## Counterexamples and do not use conditions
Present contrary evidence or a plausible disconfirming case. Explicitly state where the intended experience would be harmed by this recipe.

## Playtest
Define comparison, controls, measures, confounds, participants and what result would make the team reject or adapt the technique. Do not report a proposed test as performed.

## Claim ledger and sources
For each claim give class, source locator, applicability and confidence rationale; follow with complete reference records.

## Related entries and history
Link topics, genres, complementary/competing patterns and contradiction IDs. Record review and changes.
