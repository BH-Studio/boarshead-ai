## Genre quality and adversarial assessment

**Shooter** provides genuinely custom analysis of acquisition, tracking, prediction, discrimination, tool role, collision model, confirmation, input configuration and encounter context. Camera, military theme and kill scoring are facets rather than definitions. The broad working membership test is explicitly a heuristic; it should not become an exclusive canonical taxonomy without coordination. The missing contemporary arcade and comparative third-person evidence is accurately disclosed.

**Tactical shooter** adds information available before commitment, planning versus execution, role contribution, recoverability and nonvoice communication. It avoids equating lethality, slow movement, realism or equipment complexity with depth. Its short-TTK logic is conditional on otherwise fixed circumstances, while the behavioral alternatives remain hypotheses. The examples and guidance stay inside fictional game design rather than real-world tactical training.

**Extraction game** has a distinct excursion/exposure/retention analysis and a developed nonshooting worked example. It excludes ordinary learning and decorative evacuation from the definition and admits neighboring survival/roguelite structures. Benefits of loss, camping counterplay, persistent advantage and re-entry are not treated as settled. Voluntary stopping, asymmetric team burdens and actual attainable roles are evaluated independently of retention metrics.

**Impact-feedback pattern** adds temporal association, late/rejected/partial results, anchoring and lifetime to P02's broader state and modality distinctions. The narrower specialization merits retention rather than a duplicate generic cue pattern. Its strongest counterexample is already present: one immediate authoritative cue can be better. It does not demand that every internal state be displayed or reveal legitimately hidden outcomes.

**Baseline-loadout pattern** adds material lifecycle and baseline task viability to F02 recovery and F07 informed participation. Free queue entry is insufficient; a defined consequential role matters. It explicitly permits finite campaigns or communal reconstruction to reject the pattern. It does not promise equal duel strength, equal profit, healthier engagement or an exploit-proof economy. Its strongest unresolved issue is whether the nominal floor actually enables useful participation without shifting unacceptable burden to others.

The illustrative examples are labeled; recipes have prerequisites, practical steps, simpler alternatives, failure/exit conditions and proposed falsification. No fixed TTK, cooldown, cue-count, input or UI threshold is falsely universalized. Claim confidence is scoped, the theory/practitioner/empirical boundaries remain visible, and commercial success supplies no causal proof.
