## Primary-source findings

- **Latency:** the original study supports the 36-person, three-perspective, stationary aiming context and zero/150 ms added-delay comparison. The baseline is nonzero: 47.75 ms (SD 13.12), estimated from 20 captures. The draft correctly treats these as experimental conditions rather than player-setting thresholds or early-intent buffer guidance. [Halbhuber et al., §§3.2–4](https://epub.uni-regensburg.de/55325/1/3611070.pdf)
- **Amplification:** Model A's 678-person comparison and negative effectance/competence results, with no significant direct enjoyment/free-choice-playtime effect, support the draft's narrow counterexample. The amplified-only Model B description versus n=1699 label discrepancy is present. No code audit resolves it; retaining the caveat is appropriate. [Kao et al., §§3.7, 4.2–4.3](https://people.csail.mit.edu/dkao/pdf/3613904.3642656.pdf)
- **Haptics:** 38 recruited/36 analyzed and the six-condition iPhone design check out. Haptic presence affected selected experience constructs; low/high variants did not significantly differ on those constructs except HX expressivity. The draft preserves the unvalidated HX-instrument warning and avoids amplitude-only or equivalence conclusions. [Singhal and Schneider, §§6.3–6.5](https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/bitstreams/723405dd-f236-4aab-96f2-c1db3491e37e/download)
- **Redundancy:** the publisher table labels its higher-accuracy rows Interrupted while prose reports interruption lowering accuracy and a larger paired-haptic benefit under interruption. The inconsistency is real in the retrieved artifact. Withholding condition-specific magnitudes is justified; neither swapping labels nor declaring the experiment invalid is warranted. [Moon et al., Results/Table 1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01924/full)
- **Mechanisms/guidance:** Steam describes distinct mouse-like/stick-like action representations and action origins; PlayMaker describes time-window/consumption buffering. The draft appropriately declines their generic timing suggestions and avoids claiming runtime verification. XAG 107 distinguishes remapping from speed/complexity/duration; XAG 105/110 support configurable audio/haptic routes, not universal comfort thresholds. [Steam](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/steam_controller/getting_started_for_devs?l=english), [PlayMaker](https://docs.hutonggames.com/actions/input-actions/buffered-input/), [XAG 107](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107), [XAG 105](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/105), [XAG 110](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/110)

## Practical depth and remaining limits

Strong points include remapping bootstrap/recovery, context conflicts, stale input versus fresh destructive confirmation, required-fact timing, mono direction loss, and separating access from affect.
