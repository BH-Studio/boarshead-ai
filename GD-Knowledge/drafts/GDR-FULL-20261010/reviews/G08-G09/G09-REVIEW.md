## Evidence, logic and genre boundaries

### 4X

The definition does not turn the mnemonic into four obligatory actions or silently equate nonconquest with non-extraction. The real-time example, persistent polity/session distinction and caution about historical representation support useful separation from generic Strategy, management and grand strategy. Its proposed promises and endgame interventions remain hypotheses. Conditional snowball links are explicit, allowing upkeep, delayed benefits, logistics and coalition responses to interrupt compounding advantage. No unsupported universal catch-up or automatic early-ending rule is present.

The largest evidence weakness is concentration in one developer lineage. Three Old World accounts remain one creative context, and the interpretive papers do not independently validate its endgame mechanics. Long-session human evidence, current victory-rule feasibility and cultural consultation are still material gaps.

### Tower defense

Route topology, placement freedom, direct intervention and timing are treated as separate axes. The file does not conflate free placement with maze construction or passivity with genre membership. Forecasts distinguish known rules, announced threats and unknown results. Refund logic correctly separates currency, elapsed time, revealed information and lost opportunity. Role-sensitive upgrade comparisons allow intentional replacement rather than requiring permanent viability of every tower.

The NEAT study is used appropriately as a narrow original comparison and an inference limit. Non-significance is not converted to equality, and schedule differences remain alternative explanations. Proposed adaptive-wave and recovery interventions are not certified by that study. Current sell/routing exceptions and actual access outcomes remain open.

### Auto-battler

Preparation-versus-resolution is a useful center without requiring a shared shop or multiplayer. The solo, asynchronous, synchronous and solo-versus-Duos distinctions are careful. Four uncertainty locations are separated, and complete-state determinism is not equated with player predictability. The illustrative two-stage sampling argument is explicitly conditional; an old tier table is not advertised as current unit odds or a guarantee after repeated rerolls.

The optionality example is logically bounded: if the old options and costs remain available while a useful extra choice is added, best available value can improve; realized benefit depends on information, selection and distribution. It does not estimate a commercial advantage or infer harm. The Ludus all-tie example directly challenges single-metric parity claims, while acquisition and human experience remain outside the simulator result.

### New resolution-debrief pattern

The strongest risk is unwarranted explanatory authority: a polished factual trace can still encourage hindsight blame, false certainty or hidden-information leakage. The recipe materially addresses this with version/context capture, precommitment-versus-postresolution knowledge, missing-data exits, feasible alternatives, and no guaranteed counterfactual. Same-seed replay is correctly insufficient when the intervention changes later stochastic or agent behavior.

The whole benefit chain remains low-confidence and unvalidated. Sources 020 and 021 provide an interface existence example and a measurement counterexample; neither is a human debrief study. Proposed comparisons separately measure warranted attribution, false certainty, feasible revision, access and time burden. Simpler counters, logs, replay and omission remain genuine alternatives. This is adequate as a testable draft, not efficacy evidence.
