## Genre and pattern decisions
Cozy game is treated as an experience promise/market label, not a farming checklist or demographic essence. The demand audit distinguishes desired reasoning, loss, pace, care and expression. Optional challenges can still impose a valued reward tradeoff; fixed contracts and omission are genuine alternatives. Difficult subjects and elective strategy are documentary counterexamples to a narrow definition, not evidence of universal comfort. No personality-based moral ranking or therapy claim is adopted.

Educational game keeps explicit external objectives separate from entertainment form and efficacy. Game score, assessed understanding, retention, near transfer, external transfer and engagement are not substituted for each other. The original studies have different populations, comparators, support and outcomes, so their results do not establish contradictory universal laws. F01's corrected probe logic is faithfully adapted: changed answers under the same principle, permission versus preference, and access support versus answer cues. The mixture example is arithmetically discriminating. A direct lesson remains a serious alternative.

Idle/incremental separates automated progression from reinvestment, and background activity from return calculation and network availability. Historical taxonomy conventions are attributed rather than universalized. Prestige is optional. Growth arithmetic does not establish depth, enjoyment or wellbeing. The rate/capacity worked example yields 8 versus 4 units at four minutes, and 12 versus 20 at twenty, under its stated simplified assumptions; preference remains distinct from comprehension.

Away-state reconciliation is provisionally retained as a distinct bounded hypothesis: reconstruction from baseline through settled transitions to current state, rather than generic status, relearning or consent. Its reliable-accounting and authority prerequisites, unknown-versus-zero distinction, losses/consumption, shared-world limits and omission counterexample are substantive. The Melvor preview verifies only calculation-status feedback, not the complete recipe's benefit.
