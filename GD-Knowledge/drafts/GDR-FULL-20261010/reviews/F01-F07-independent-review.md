## Evidence labels, contradictions and practical use

Claim classes generally track actual support. Original patterns are explicitly unvalidated hypotheses; source-reported modeling/practitioner accounts are not new observations. Confidence reasons and source access/independence are unusually explicit. F01 PAT-01 C-04 mixes the vocabulary result and its motivational bridge under an empirical label; optional cleanup is to split the source result from the inference, since the surrounding prose already distinguishes them. F07 documentary correction classified as empirical does not imply new participants, and its qualifier makes that clear.

All three patterns include actionable recipes, prerequisites, adjustable variables, simpler alternatives, explicit exits, costs, failure signs and proposed falsifiers. The strongest recommendation challenges are substantive. No universal sample size, loop duration, mastery threshold, play-time safety limit or quantified production saving was invented.

No substantive F01-versus-F07 contradiction found: both separate continuation from wellbeing, preserve accommodations, distinguish chosen challenge from unwanted pressure, and acknowledge costs to teammates when exiting. Agency wording differs slightly (perceived consequential capacity versus understandable usable choices); use a shared vocabulary crosswalk without pretending either operational definition is a formal standard. F01's optional discovery route and XAG tutorial guidance concern different outcomes; retain F01-X-06 rather than manufacture an inconsistency.
