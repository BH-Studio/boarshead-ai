## Findings by candidate
- MMO: continuing shared relationships and institutions are a useful operational boundary, with persistence distinct from uninterrupted co-presence and MMORPG combat. Seasonal/shard qualifications avoid treating a lobby/account as a world. Offline progression and standing exchange are properly scoped asynchronous subsystems. Roles, attribution, absence, progression, economy access, re-entry, content cadence and operating obligations are substantively addressed. Mandatory collaboration remains a valid chosen promise; neither scarcity nor optional play is universally preferred. World retirement, population engineering and contemporary intervention evidence remain open.
- MOBA: the decision-family boundary is broad but explicitly contested and sharpened by base/score endings, shared/individual resources and unusual control arrangements. Objectives, role opportunities, conversion, escalation, trailing-team decisions, information provenance, communication and moderation evidence are distinct. Pings do not replace negotiation or prove safety; reports and poor performance do not establish misconduct. Draft/ban, role queues, current competitive rules and intervention coverage remain open rather than falsely comprehensive.
- Battle royale: terminal unit/eligibility distinguishes defeat, rescue prerequisite, pending return, participation and finality. Shooter spatial pressure is separated from puzzle elimination; literal last-survivor victory is not asserted sufficient for every genre case. Acquisition, route uncertainty, pressure/reachability, scouting, return burdens and voluntary spectatorship receive conditional alternatives and counterexamples. No ideal circle, player count or guarantee that recovery reduces frustration is invented.
- Public Pressure, Private Position: retain provisional draft. Its joint constraint/opportunity/information authoring is more than P02 cue redundancy: a fully accessible forecast can still offer no feasible response. Prerequisites correctly exclude intended schedule-discovery, false precision and private-state disclosure. Dependence on F02/F06/W03/P02 requires owner/duplication resolution before canonical allocation; no efficacy proof.
- Bounded Return Before Final Elimination: retain provisional draft. Explicit recovery-to-terminal state contract, cancellation/interruption and useful re-entry add content beyond generic safe exit. Scope excludes ordinary open-world revival and respawn without finality. Distinct from G19 post-elimination participation: G21 permits renewed active eligibility before terminal exclusion; G19 permits valued, constrained participation after elimination without requiring restoration. They share information, voluntary-exit and social-pressure concerns and should be cross-linked during coordination, not automatically merged.
