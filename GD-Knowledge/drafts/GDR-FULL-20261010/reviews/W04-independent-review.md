## Content challenge and useful boundaries

1. Recovery: the pattern is worth retaining as a bounded hypothesis because it distinguishes availability of a named activity from a quiet-looking interval. It also permits fixed transitions, explicit pause, lower continuous pressure and no interval. Its strongest unresolved risk is that state qualification creates delay or falsely models mental state. The draft directly addresses both and gives local rejection criteria. Do not present it as a validated adaptive pacing algorithm.
2. Narrative interfaces: the occurrence-versus-acknowledgment problem is distinct from W02's selected speech act. Actor attribution, expiry, legitimate repetition, no-selection and restore horizons provide substantive additional guidance rather than a renamed generic checklist. Correct silence, purposeful amnesia and unreliable perspective are genuine counterexamples. The author trace is explicitly not a meaning oracle.
3. Theme: intent, artifact, interpretation and empirical reception remain separate. The civic-archive example provides usable alternative designs without claiming canon or observations. Disagreement is not failed comprehension; intentional dissonance does not excuse defects. Representation safeguards are proposals rather than legal standards, universal consent or causal wellbeing conclusions.
4. Topic coverage: encounters cover escalation, contrast, alternatives/nonviolence, exits, revisit, density denominators and resource dependencies. Interfaces cover state, event, selection, continuity and diagnostics. Theme covers alignment, dissonance, tone, symbols, representation and openness. Depth is uneven but openly reported: tone/symbolism and cultural review lack comparative evidence; recovery and authoring benefits remain untested. This is adequate draft coverage, not taxonomy closure.

All recipes include practical conditions, alternatives, costs and disconfirming proposed studies. The corrected F01/W02 transfer distinction is preserved: changed underlying rules are adaptation, not same-rule transfer. Nothing encountered claims actual playtests, engine checks, consultation or direct game observations. No numerical accessibility standard is imported.
