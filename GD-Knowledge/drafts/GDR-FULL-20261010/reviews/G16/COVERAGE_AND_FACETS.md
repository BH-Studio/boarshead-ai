# G16 coverage and facet audit

Roster authority: historical full-run coordination plan, G16 row and rows38–40, plus TAXONOMY.md Genre library/Cross-domain facets. Coverage labels are obligations, not empirical categories. No new genre or child ID is allocated.

| Assigned genre | Distinct bounded contribution | Explicit unresolved coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Roguelike, G16-GEN-01 | Declared historical/systemic profile; stable rules in variable situations; consequential attempt boundaries; knowledge versus stored progress. | Console/cultural traditions, failed designs, actual generator distributions/build behavior, longitudinal transfer and target access populations. |
| Roguelite, G16-GEN-02 | Multiple-attempt retention and its purpose; power/opportunity/narrative/knowledge separation; incentive and attribution analysis. | Actual progression economies, longitudinal effects, dropouts, cooperative/competitive accounts and direct affected-player representation input. |
| Deckbuilder, G16-GEN-03 | In-play composition, zones/timing/cycles, ordered versus randomized rules, information rights and multiple goal horizons. | Authentic/visual base Aeon rules, actual digital adaptations, metagame datasets, lexical localization and direct outcome studies. |

The split is analytical and permits overlap. It neither defines roguelite as easy nor upgrades as necessary; card battlers/collectible collections do not automatically become deckbuilders. One-pass designs can belong to the genre while failing the circulating-deck pattern's prerequisites.

| Required facet | Treatment and review result |
|---|---|
| Player goal | Adaptable planning, discovery, self-contained achievement, accumulation, continuity, composition and lexical play are separated; no universal optimal goal. |
| Experience | Mastery, wanted uncertainty, return burden, collection pleasure and agency are hypotheses/declared goals; no blanket retention or enjoyment effect. |
| Problem | Misunderstood resets, stale knowledge, invalid generation, unwanted repetitive labor, delayed/irrelevant rewards and information leakage are distinct diagnoses. |
| Mechanic | Generation, resource commitment, selective persistence, unlock pools, draw/discard/retention, removal and exceptions have concrete conditional models. |
| Modality | Discrete versus continuous action and physical versus digital card interaction are distinguished; audiovisual/assistive implementations untested. |
| Platform | Historical/versioned documents are identified. No current build/port equivalence, universal device threshold or performance certification. |
| Input | Commands, reaction demands, card selection/inspection and physical shuffling are separate demands. Alternate routes require actual task testing. |
| Audience/access need | Ordinary accommodations persist separately from earned upgrades; prior familiarity, literacy/language, motor/perceptual needs and session constraints remain independent. Consultation/testing absent. |
| Session model | Attempt, suspend, abandonment, re-entry, between-run continuity, finite narrative, solo/cooperative/competitive implications present. Current shared-session dependency review still needed. |
| Production constraint | Generator constraints/versioned seeds, save migration, duplicate rewards, branching narrative, card interactions, localization and verification costs are acknowledged without cost estimates. |
| Evidence strength | Documentary rules/claims, practitioner rationale, conditional inference and untested intervention chains are labeled; one narrow external experiment is not genre-wide validation. |

No facet is a second folder hierarchy. Complete headings are not complete research. G16-QA/QB/QC/QD are not allocated IDs: the author's Q-A to Q-D remain local queued proposals only.
