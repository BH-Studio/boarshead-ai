## Independent content challenge

1. Fighting versus beat-em-up: distinct organizing problems survive comparison. Fighting emphasizes differentiated repertoires, conditional opportunity and adaptation; beat-em-up emphasizes crowd-role relationships, spatial control, resource access and route/cooperative continuity. Boss duels and wave modes are countercases, not reasons to force mutually exclusive labels. Arena, camera, player count, ranking and violence are not treated as universal membership tests.
2. RPG versus action RPG versus crawler: role authorship/resolution/recognition, real-time execution/configuration interaction, and spatial/expedition dependency are genuinely distinct. These remain broad working scopes; they should not silently become canonical necessary-and-sufficient definitions. Fixed roles, nonviolent examples, straight upgrades and intentionally terminal expeditions remain legitimate alternatives.
3. Replay-to-variant practice: retain as a narrow specialization of F01 because replay provenance, restoration and continuation validity add a real problem. The draft delegates the general pedagogy, preserves accommodations, separates prediction from preference and permits authored drills instead. Altering a decisive state under the same rules tests transfer; introducing a new ruleset would require separately labeled adaptation. Full fidelity and durable benefit remain unvalidated.
4. Recoverable expenditure window: retain separately from W04's usable recovery interval and G04's viable re-entry resources. Voluntary spending creates its outstanding recoverable amount; injury, exit, revival and material participation are different contracts. The 10/3/2 arithmetic is internally consistent, explicitly illustrative, and not a balance recommendation. The strongest unresolved issue is whether recovery creates a worthwhile choice rather than mandatory chasing or ally pressure.
5. Dependency-complete loadout switch: retain separately from remapping, safe exit and G04's baseline re-entry kit. It validates the current dependencies of a saved preference, allows explicit partial application, rejects an invalid prior state as a universally safe fallback, and does not promise resource resets. The documented ingredients do not prove the assembled benefit. Simplifying dependencies or manual editing may be better.
6. Coverage is adequate for bounded drafts, not complete genre coverage. The files explicitly retain current-build, access-population, long-term learning, party interaction, exact revival, generated-layout, regional/language and missing-owner gaps. No performed tests, empirical benefits, numerical defaults or studio decisions were fabricated.
