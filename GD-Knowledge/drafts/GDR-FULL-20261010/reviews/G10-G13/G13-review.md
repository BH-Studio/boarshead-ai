# G13 independent review

Decision: retain three genres and two patterns unchanged as 0.1.0 drafts.

## Content findings

Colony simulation emphasizes continuing delegated community operation without requiring maximal person detail or a population threshold. Life simulation emphasizes persistent everyday lives and distinguishes player goals, character wishes, constraints and incentives. Sandbox emphasizes player-defined projects over reusable affordances, separately from open-world geography or absence of rules. Their overlapping boundaries are explicit and do not collapse the roster.

Scoped autonomous changes distinguishes permission, feasibility, selection and commitment; it handles inherited settings, pending events, save/reset and shared authority. Reversible experiment branch requires an explicit protected state and rule-fidelity contract. It cannot erase knowledge, reverse external actions or silently merge histories. Both are full practical proposals with simpler alternatives, costs, counterexamples and falsifiers, not validated effects.

Historical identity/language limitations are dated and do not become claims about current support. No therapeutic, creativity, empathy, educational or wellbeing effect is inferred from creator descriptions.

## Fresh primary checks

- [EA Neighborhood Stories](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/neighborhood-stories-system), configuration/recent stories: confirms group and household scopes, per-save configurations and retrospective event access. It does not prove user comprehension or runtime enforcement.
- [EA orientation design account](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/sexual-orientation-in-the-sims-4), feature/FAQ: supports the historical distinction between attraction parameters, pronouns and architecture. The candidate preserves the 2022 context.
- [Klei Occupational Upgrade announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=457140&enddate=1521752728), 8 February 2018: explicitly reports reverting the preview priority system after testing. This is a documented negative production case, not a controlled player-effect study.
- [Bay 12, 13 September 2013](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2013.html): supports the caution that highlighting extreme personality facets can overstate a character description. No measured player response follows.
- [Nintendo, Being the Mayor](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-Animal-Crossing-New-Leaf/Animal-Crossing-New-Leaf/2-Being-the-Mayor/2-Being-the-Mayor-738645.html): confirms shop-closure rationale and the dejected response after declining a ceremony. Mechanical refusal and emotionally neutral framing remain distinct; universal harm is not claimed.
- [Minecraft feature-testing guide](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/testing-new-minecraft-features), Bedrock experiment steps: confirms creation of a copy for the described existing-world route. This is version testing, not general gameplay rollback or proof of complete isolation.
- [Factorio #412](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-412), undo information/improvements: provides a historical production precedent; announced facilities are not current runtime reproduction.

## Open questions

The corpus is creator-heavy, with no newly inspected participant datasets. Missing areas include low-agent and aggregate/individual hybrids, nonviolent/low-scarcity range, virtual pets/farming/mobile offline care, actual locale flows, multiplayer governance, novice creation, mod/programming sandboxes, persistence failures and maintenance cost. G12/F03/F05/F06/W04/P05 transfers need their own selected dependency review.

The relation of G13 autonomous changes to G12/G08 delegation and F06 shared-state permission concepts needs Coordination. G13 experiment branches differ from G11 read-only rereading but share persistence/authority concerns. Preserve all identities and speculative novelty status.
