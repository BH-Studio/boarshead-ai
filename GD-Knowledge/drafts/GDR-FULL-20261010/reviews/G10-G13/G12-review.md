## Content findings

Simulation separates model, interface, correspondence/fidelity, prediction envelope, learning goal and experience goal. Management adds sustained allocation, scheduling, delegation and diagnosis. City builder adds location, networks, service distribution and settlement obligations. These are meaningful conditional differences, not renamed shared-system summaries. Townscaper and Terra Nil are bounded counterexamples, not universal genre arbiters.

Pending-work reconciliation distinguishes current shortage, existing commitments, deadlines and blocked work, while allowing intentional redundancy. Its arithmetic illustration is valid: two available plus four on-time parts meets a six-part deadline; four late parts do not. Bounded delegation preserves retained decisions and separates stopping new work from undoing current execution. Both count configuration/inspection burden and offer simpler alternatives. They do not establish player benefit.

Model-generated social outcomes are not presented as human laws. Construction costs, operation, service reach and welfare are not conflated. Access/units/localization and production obligations are explicit without imported universal numerical requirements.
