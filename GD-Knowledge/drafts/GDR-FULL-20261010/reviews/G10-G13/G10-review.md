# G10 independent review

Decision: retain the two genre candidates and one pattern unchanged as 0.1.0 drafts.

## Content findings

Puzzle distinguishes discovery, deduction, manipulation, planning and insight without asserting a fixed cognitive sequence or requiring one answer, no timer or universal undo. Detective/mystery adds provenance, alternative explanations, justified commitment and fictional truth rather than merely renaming puzzle. Both preserve the difference between completion and demonstrated understanding.

Evidence-bounded solution acceptance is unusually careful about its strongest claim: equivalence over the complete permitted inquiry set is not merely consistency with sampled observations. Stateful inquiries require histories; stochastic observations are excluded from the simple deterministic claim. The formal two-cell illustration is valid under its stated assumptions. Wording equivalence, multiple valid constructions and distinct hidden histories are separated. No blanket acceptance of plausible accusations is recommended.

Recipes include explicit exit conditions, simpler alternatives and falsifiers. Access adaptations retain facts and uncertainty without promising equal difficulty or emotion. Existing G03 help and W03 corroboration patterns are reused rather than duplicated.

- [Tatham, Black Box](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/doc/blackbox.html), acceptance paragraph and §19.1: supports output-equivalent layouts and distinguishing untried probes. The candidate does not overextend this to arbitrary narrative truth.
- [Tatham, writing a puzzle](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/devel/writing.html), §6.1/6.2: collection policy and generator guidance remain scoped practitioner evidence.
- [Pope, August 2017 devlog](https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-30/), #734 and reader #740: grouped checking, alternative fate formulations and the two-known/one-varied objection retain separate attribution. The reader objection is not reported as a tested exploit frequency.
- [Golden Idol designer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/case-of-the-golden-idol), validation/clue discussion: supports the reported iteration tradeoff, not a controlled effect or universal numerical setting.
- [Tatham, common features](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/doc/common.html), §2.1–2.2: undo/restart and version-dependent random-seed reproduction are correctly distinguished from restored ignorance.
- [Thimbleweed Park hints](https://blog.thimbleweedpark.com/hints_and_dialogs.html), old-save passage: supports fallible state reconstruction; no reported heuristic percentage is converted into a general accuracy guarantee.
- [inkle, translation explanation](https://medium.com/@inklestudios/why-does-translation-unlock-sites-in-heavens-vault-e05c4ccadf62), dated 18 November 2025: explicitly separates translation from plot progression and relates it to understanding.

- Hempuli's [jam listing](https://hempuli.itch.io/baba-is-you) and [commercial description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/736260/Baba_Is_You/) correctly support different artifact-level claims: jam undo/restart versus commercial mutable rules. They are not independent outcome studies.

Other listed product/creator routes were reopened for identity/access checking. No game was run. The lead-only learning article was not promoted to evidence.
