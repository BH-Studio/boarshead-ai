# G11 independent review

Decision: retain two genres and two patterns unchanged as 0.1.0 drafts.

## Content findings

Interactive fiction routes language-mediated contribution and recognition problems. Visual novel routes the staged reading event, performance, pacing and return behavior. Neither is reduced to branching, romance, national origin, a text percentage or a mandatory asset list. The planetarian counterexample is edition-scoped.

Intent-preserving parser repair properly stops when intention is unknown, exact wording is the task, a suggestion discloses a secret, or repair would substitute a different action. State-safe narrative rereading separates transcript, replay, rollback, locked-choice traversal and cross-play state. It explicitly treats its operation matrix as proposed contracts, not guaranteed engine behavior. Both have concrete contrasting examples and negative conditions.

The empirical discussion does not mistake nonsignificance for equivalence, short-story evidence for VN validation, encounter for comprehension, or preference for correct prediction. Four of five in the Fendt discussion refers to the five agency items; Figure 2 labels its sixth item as replay preference, outside agency. Table 2 supports the stated count at the conventional 0.05 level. No mandatory numerical correction is warranted in context.

## Fresh primary checks

- [Inform parser errors](https://ganelson.github.io/inform-website/book/WI_18_35.html): confirms distinct recognition failures and contextual error handling. Related understanding/mistakes sections were reopened.
- [Ren'Py rollback](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/save_load_rollback.html), fixed rollback and custom-screen caveats, and [persistent data](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/persistent.html), opening: confirm distinct state scopes and implementation caveats. The current documentation banner is 8.5.4.
- [IFTF 2019 report](https://accessibility.iftechfoundation.org/), methods/sources: 34 respondents and two specially prepared games, with recommendations combining observations and committee/APX expertise. It is not representative proof that one IF platform is universally superior.
- [planetarian Nintendo publisher description](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/planetarian-the-reverie-of-a-little-planet-and-snow-globe-switch/), description and release fields: confirms VN naming, no choice-based branching and 27 June 2024 combined edition.
- [Fendt et al.](https://www.cs.uky.edu/~sgware/reading/papers/fendt2012achieving.pdf), Figure 2, Results/Table 2: confirms the selected 52 of 79 branching participants and 54/44 comparison groups. Selection, demographic and single-session caveats remain essential.
- [Cardona-Rivera et al.](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/download/12716/12564/16233), Method/Results: 88 adults, 95% with over five years of gaming, fixed-order six choices and retrospective ratings. No all-five-scales or unrestricted causality claim is introduced.
- IFTF terminology, Dreamhold, Ink, Ren'Py history/NVL/preferences/voice/translation/self-voicing, Scarlet Hollow and Emily Short routes were additionally reopened for identity/access and targeted context; this is not a fresh full manual or all-version audit.
