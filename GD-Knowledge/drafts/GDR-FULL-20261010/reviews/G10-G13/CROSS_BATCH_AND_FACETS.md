## Cross-batch pattern docket

1. **G08-PAT-02 bounded-standing-orders versus G12-PAT-02 bounded-delegation-contract.** The G08 reviewer raised overlap through the parent; this reviewer also inspected the captured G08 purpose, prerequisites and recipe. Both retain a valued decision while defining scope, resources, conditions, exceptions, inspection and termination. This is substantive overlap, not merely similar names. G08 emphasizes standing orders, information permissions and manual override/resumption; G12 emphasizes policy supervision, pending delivery and stop/cancel semantics. Coordination should decide a shared core versus genre adaptations or a future consolidation. Preserve exact IDs and source aliases; no merge is enacted. The inspected front matter identifies GDR-CAND-20261010-G08-PAT-02; its exact captured hash is recorded below.

2. **G12-PAT-02 versus G13-PAT-01 scoped-autonomous-changes.** Delegation authorizes execution of a chosen policy; autonomous-change scope governs which categories of persistent world events may occur. The latter explicitly separates permission from feasibility and event selection and addresses inherited group/individual rules. This is a plausible conditional distinction, not proven novelty. Review alongside G08 and relevant F06 permission patterns before permanent allocation.

3. **G11-PAT-02 state-safe-narrative-rereading versus G13-PAT-02 reversible-experiment-branch.** Rereading covers viewing/replaying prior material and returning with specified state effects; experiment branching deliberately changes a separate trial state while protecting an original. Shared persistence/knowledge/authority constraints are not independent evidence of either's efficacy. Retain separate draft recipes unless the central owner decides to consolidate shared prerequisites.

4. **G10-PAT-01 evidence-bounded-solution-acceptance versus W02-PAT-02 multi-route-objective-contract.** G10's added issue is equivalence over the complete permitted observation space versus currently sampled consistency, plus expression recognition. W02 owns the broader outcome/conditions/acceptance/reward distinction. Keep the narrow contribution without reallocating W02's concepts.

5. **G11-PAT-01 intent-preserving-parser-repair versus W02-PAT-01 intent-and-response-contract.** G11 handles recognition/clarification before commitment; W02 follows a selected action/utterance into performance and response. This division is plausible but central ownership remains unresolved.

6. **G12-PAT-01 pending-work-reconciliation.** The unit/horizon/commitment distinction is useful and not equivalent to an economy conversion-loop exploit check. It is shared Foundations work with genre adaptations; current registry novelty is not established.
