## Content decision
Survival crafting separates material identity, physical access, quantity, conversion capacity and permission, including last-tool/bootstrap and post-loss states. It does not turn hunger, combat, repair or multiplayer into membership requirements. The broader Survival parent and sandbox/automation boundaries remain pending cross-batch integration.

Soulslike is appropriately a contested lineage/family-resemblance analysis. Sekiro is a comparator rather than a settled membership verdict. Retry transit, expedition attrition, build commitment and encounter execution remain separable analytic questions, with integrated expeditions retained as a contrary case. Required access information does not fade as optional teaching support does. Assistance is neither excluded by definition nor advertised as identical challenge.

Discovery-scoped resource survey is provisionally worth retaining as a narrow dynamic-query pattern. It explicitly separates known identity, geographic coverage, disclosure precision and current versus historical state, including negative results and indirect leaks. Its production precedent is one system in two versions, not replicated efficacy. A correctly filtered query can still reveal an unknown place or another player's activity; the pattern states this counterexample and can be rejected. Before permanent allocation compare it with G02 known-space recovery and P02 cue alternatives. Merge or demote to an adaptation if coordination finds no distinct retrieval need.
