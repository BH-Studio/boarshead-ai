- GEN-01 Collectible card game: substantively separates persistent functional access, pre-match construction, limited-event pools and in-run deckbuilding. Covers priority/tempo, response information side channels, format/card revisions, re-entry, monetization pressure, audience variance, localization and production dependencies. The entitlement maximum model correctly avoids summing owned and loaned playsets. Independent arithmetic verification gives 0.42707079549 for its stated hypergeometric example. No necessary correction.
- GEN-02 Board-game adaptation: explicitly a source-edition relationship rather than a single mechanic. Separates rules fidelity, preserved discretionary choices, information access and social outcome. Automation, private/public commitment, undo, simultaneous conflicts, asynchronous clocks, notification versus acknowledgement, social negotiation, teaching, accessibility, errata and deliberate variants receive usable custom guidance. Exact original/digital matched conformance remains unavailable; the draft does not disguise this. No necessary correction.
- PAT-01 Access-normalized trial pool: retain provisionally. Useful distinct entitlement/snapshot/expiry workflow; neither a generic learning pattern nor proof that equal inventories produce fairness. Adequate prerequisites, smallest-pool alternative, supplied-deck contrast, refusability, ownership-boundary probes and rejection criteria. Production examples demonstrate possibility, not treatment efficacy.
- PAT-02 Information-bounded undo: retain provisionally. Adds materially useful disclosure/dependency analysis to F07 safe-exit guidance: public intent before randomness, fixed-seed scouting, partner commitments, clocks and uncertain acknowledgement. Properly distinguishes teaching hindsight as a declared variant and does not universalize a no-undo policy. Canonical overlap/owner decision still required; no merge or new identity authorized.
