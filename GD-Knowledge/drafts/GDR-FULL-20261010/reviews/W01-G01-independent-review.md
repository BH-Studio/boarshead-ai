## Topic and genre quality

- **Narrative structures:** Distinguishes linear chronology, authored branching, systemic selection and emergent interpretation; separates influence from option/ending counts. Fixed participation and minimal narrative are serious alternatives. The delayed-relay example carries consequences past the choice rather than merely promising a callback. Replay trust, long-horizon consequences and production benefit remain research gaps.
- **Worldbuilding:** Covers rules, history, cultures, institutions, economies, geography and coherence through practical questions. Distinguishes operational facts, traces, attributed claims and unresolved interpretation. The gate-dispute example connects dimensions without supplying game canon; intentional impossible geography and optional richness counter an overly administrative model. Systemic histories/transmedia remain lead-only gaps; no ethnographic or economic authority is claimed.
- **Character design:** Separates goal intelligibility, liking, autonomy and interpretation. Includes belief, constraints, relationships, refusal, stable commitments, nonverbal and intentionally unknowable characters. It does not impose trauma or transformation. Avatar projection, collective/multiplayer agency and long-term relationships are less evidenced than the practical authored-scene framework.
- **Consequence-preserving reconvergence:** Distinct operational recipe with explicit incompatible-history exits. A resource promise cannot be repaired by a tonal callback. Production savings are not presumed. This is a useful adaptation of branch-and-bottleneck practice, not a novel discovery or experimentally validated package.
- **Survival horror:** Organizes guidance around exposure, expenditure, alternatives and recovery. Scarcity is relative to competing uses, not low inventory totals. Armed, unarmed and systemic-tool play remain possible. Retry-supported recovery does not ban deliberate permadeath. The resource/route worked example and inaccessible-chase counterexample are specific and useful.
- **Psychological horror:** Organizes guidance around evidence, interpretation, identity/responsibility and viewpoint. Explicitly separates ambiguity from confusion, mental illness from genre, and usable controls from explaining hidden systems. Reliable narration, known outcomes and competent combat remain possible. The memorial example and modality/translation disclosure audit are materially different from survival guidance.
- **Configurable threat consequences:** Separates presentation/contact, harm/penalty, progression/recovery and retained activity. It includes defeated-state dependencies, nonlethal obstruction and mode-description semantics. A targeted assist or unchanged chosen stakes can be better than a separate mode. Nonlethal does not mean emotionally comfortable or equally frightening.

The genre documents are not generic checklist copies: their custom research questions, core loops, examples, failure diagnoses and proposed comparisons differ. Shared access/production constraints are legitimate overlap. Their boundaries are proposed, overlapping routing conventions, not validated universal genre definitions. Cooperative/VR play, audience prevalence, independent reception, cultural variation, long-term replay and failed productions are disclosed or sensible future gaps, not grounds to declare every category researched.

Checks were relevant-passage inspections, not full bibliographic re-audits or reanalyses. No source assets are redistributed.
