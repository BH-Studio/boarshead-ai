## Content findings

### W03

- **Environmental storytelling:** clear separation of reachable evidence, perceived relation, interpretation and use. Testimony, trace and creator intent are distinct. Corroboration is not a clue quota; repeated testimony is not independent support. Unreliable fiction does not silently license inconsistent interaction rules. The memorial counterexample genuinely rejects a reconstruction requirement.
- **Level and spatial design:** topology, geometry, exposure and learned understanding stay separate. A sightline is not an entrance; a landmark asset is not proof of orientation. Route learning, completion speed and survey knowledge are not interchangeable. The empirical section correctly avoids turning experimental map-landmark conditions into world-density targets.
- **World structure and exploration:** hubs/open worlds are organizational descriptions, not quality guarantees. Density is decomposed usefully; changed travel is not automatically worthwhile, and unchanged travel is not automatically waste. Knowledge, recognition, reachability and chosen intention remain distinct. Re-entry efficacy is explicitly untested.
- **Corroborated spatial inference:** sufficiently actionable and explicitly original synthesis. Rival explanations, information-equivalent access, continuity, simple alternatives and exit conditions prevent a generic “add clues” recipe. Strongest objection remains that additional evidence can impose unwanted closure; the text allows omission.
- **Destination-signaled route choice:** separates destination, actual connection/commitment and discovery content. The unnamed-passage example demonstrates that the recipe can withhold a destination. A linear route or aimless wandering can be preferable. Its full efficacy remains a hypothesis rather than borrowed authority from Outer Wilds.

### G03

All three genre files contain genuinely custom conditional guidance, not renamed generic checklists. The broad adventure family is not reduced to point-and-click or defined by excluding all action.

- **Action adventure:** examines sequential, embedded and loose coupling of arrival, inference and execution; it does not require combat. It preserves the countercase in which separating action from observation destroys the intended task. Bypass is not equivalent puzzle participation.
- **Adventure:** concerns evidence, interpretation, discovery order and knowledge versus authorization/flags. Correct knowledge need not require a particular clue order when knowledge is actually the gate; a signed authorization can legitimately require an artifact. The ambiguous-story countercase prevents deduction from becoming universal.
- **Point-and-click:** distinguishes detecting, selecting, expressing a verb, satisfying state and interpreting feedback. Mouse hardware is not mandatory. Touch/controller alternatives are proposals, not parity claims. Hotspot reveals can remove intended search, while selection access is not automatically a solution spoiler. Dialogue-state and inventory-loss cases give meaningful subgenre specificity.
- **Recoverable dependency gate:** a contract/state audit, not a prohibition on loss or irreversible endings. Technical reachability and understandable evidence are checked separately. The pattern name remains a retrieval risk: surface its stated definition prominently so “recoverable” is not returned as unlimited reversibility.
- **Player-requested state-aware hints:** distinct from F01 teaching/transfer. It permits direct answers on request, rejects fixed mandatory hint ladders, and exits when state is untrusted. Its strongest risk is that even selecting a help goal can reveal a hidden question; the recipe explicitly addresses that risk. Proposed comparisons account for help-user self-selection.

Claim classifications and confidence are generally well calibrated. G03's observed-example labels for documentation explicitly concern inspected text artifacts, not gameplay; preserve that qualifier in any export. W03's corrigendum classification does not inflate its dataset count. Synthetic examples and protocols remain labeled; no fabricated participant outcomes, implementation correctness or host tests found.
