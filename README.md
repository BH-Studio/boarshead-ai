# Boarshead AI

## About this repository

I'm Doug Evans, the developer behind Boar’s Head Studio. This repository contains the AI prompts, custom GPT instructions, reference materials, and workflows I’m developing and using to support my work.

The current collection focuses on game design, narrative and worldbuilding, game and level analysis, studio planning, and marketing. I’m sharing these resources publicly so others can explore the approach, adapt it to their own projects, and build on what they find useful.

## What I’m working toward

My goal is to make AI a more useful, structured part of the creative and development process. That means giving it clear responsibilities, relevant context, defined outputs, and checkpoints where a person reviews the work and decides what happens next.

The workflows explore how to:

- Turn broad ideas into clear requirements and practical designs.
- Examine a problem through multiple professional perspectives.
- Research existing games and extract lessons that apply to a new project.
- Challenge assumptions, identify risks, and review designs before implementation.
- Preserve narrative continuity and distinguish proposed ideas from approved canon.
- Prepare implementation-ready specifications and milestone handoffs for Codex where appropriate.
- Support the business, marketing, and community work involved in running an independent studio.

Human judgment remains central. Creative direction, scope, approvals, and final decisions stay with the person using the tools.

## How to use these resources

1. Choose the package that fits your task.
2. Read its README or setup instructions before configuring your AI tool.
3. Provide the project context and reference material the workflow needs.
4. Adapt the instructions to your goals, constraints, and preferred tools.
5. Where acceptance tests are included, use them to check the configuration.
6. Review the outputs, verify important claims, and approve decisions before moving forward.

Most of this repository consists of instructions and reference material. Setup requirements vary by package. Named teams, directors, and specialist roles describe perspectives used to guide the AI; they do not necessarily represent independently running agents.

## Status and limitations

This is a working collection that will evolve as I test ideas and refine my process. Packages may differ in maturity, and changes to AI models or tools can affect their behavior.

AI can produce errors, overlook constraints, and present uncertain conclusions confidently. Treat its outputs as material to review and validate. Inclusion here does not guarantee that a workflow will produce correct, secure, or production-ready results for your project.
