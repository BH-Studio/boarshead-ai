&#x20;  \* Player orientation

2\. \*\*Layout \& Structural Logic\*\*

&#x20;  \* Pathing and branching

&#x20;  \* Critical path vs optional space

&#x20;  \* Macro vs micro structure



3\. \*\*Wayfinding \& Navigation\*\*

&#x20;  \* Landmarks

&#x20;  \* Signposting

&#x20;  \* Player guidance (diegetic vs UI)



4\. \*\*Player Flow \& Pacing\*\*

&#x20;  \* Movement rhythm

&#x20;  \* Tension and release

&#x20;  \* Interruptions and transitions



5\. \*\*Encounter Design \& Spatial Combat\*\*

&#x20;  \* Placement of threats and opportunities

&#x20;  \* Cover, elevation, lines of sight

&#x20;  \* Spatial problem-solving



6\. \*\*Environmental Affordances\*\*

&#x20;  \* What space invites the player to do

&#x20;  \* Clarity of interactive elements

&#x20;  \* Risk vs reward positioning



7\. \*\*Challenge Scaling Through Space\*\*

&#x20;  \* Difficulty via layout, not stats

&#x20;  \* Spatial escalation

&#x20;  \* Safe zones vs pressure zones



8\. \*\*Environmental Storytelling \& Theme\*\*

&#x20;  \* Narrative communicated through space

&#x20;  \* Mood, tone, and atmosphere

&#x20;  \* World coherence



9\. \*\*Player Agency in Space\*\*

&#x20;  \* Freedom of approach

&#x20;  \* Multiple solutions

&#x20;  \* Expression through movement



10\. \*\*Repetition, Variation, and Reuse\*\*

&#x20;   \* Modular design

&#x20;   \* Spatial remixing

&#x20;   \* Fatigue vs familiarity



11\. \*\*Accessibility \& Inclusivity in Space\*\*

&#x20;   \* Readability for diverse players

&#x20;   \* Camera considerations

&#x20;   \* Navigation assistance



12\. \*\*Production-Conscious Level Design\*\*

&#x20;   \* Asset reuse

&#x20;   \* Scope control

&#x20;   \* Development efficiency
